  • What you did or said

    {{N}}.10 Reading vs. Videos / TV Part 3

    People enjoy different mediums due to personal preferences and learning styles. Reading develops concentration, imagination, and analytical skills, while videos and TV offer visual stimulation and quicker information absorption. Modern lifestyles also influence choices; busy schedules may make videos seem more convenient, whereas reading appeals to those who value reflection and depth.
