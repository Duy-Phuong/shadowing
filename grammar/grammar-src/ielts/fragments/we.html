{{N}}.3 Model Essay – Preservation of Traditional Skills Writing

Essay Prompt

In many countries, traditional skills and ways of life are dying out. Some people believe that it is pointless to try and keep them alive. To what extent do you agree or disagree with this opinion?

Model Answer (Band 7.0)

In the modern era, rapid technological advancement and globalization have led to the steady decline of traditional skills and customs. Consequently, some individuals argue that attempting to preserve these outdated ways of life is a futile endeavor. I strongly disagree with this perspective, as I believe that traditional skills are vital for maintaining cultural identity and often hold unique, practical value that modern technology cannot easily replicate.

Those who argue against preserving traditional skills often point to economic efficiency. It is undeniable that industrial machinery, automation, and digital technology can produce goods and complete tasks much faster than human hands or historical methods. For instance, mass-produced clothing and mechanized agriculture have largely replaced traditional weaving and manual farming because they can meet the demands of a growing global population at a fraction of the cost. From a strictly economic standpoint, clinging to slower, traditional methods can seem like a barrier to national progress and development.

However, viewing this issue purely through an economic lens ignores the profound cultural and historical significance of these practices. Traditional skills are a tangible link to a nation’s heritage, offering future generations an understanding of how their ancestors lived and worked. Furthermore, many historical techniques still possess highly practical, specialized applications today, particularly in conservation and heritage tourism. For example, the skills required to work with draft horses—such as using them to carefully pull up track on heritage narrow-gauge railroads—keep historical industrial techniques alive in a way that heavy, modern machinery could easily damage. These practices not only provide sustainable alternatives but also enrich the cultural landscape, drawing tourism and educational interest.

In conclusion, while it is true that modern technology has made many old ways of working obsolete in mainstream industry, it is far from pointless to keep traditional skills alive. Preserving these practices is essential for safeguarding cultural history and maintaining specialized techniques that continue to offer unique value in the modern world.

{{N}}.4 Model Essay – Driverless Vehicles Writing

IELTS Writing Task 2

In the future, all cars, buses, and trucks will be driverless. The only people traveling inside these vehicles will be passengers. Do you think the advantages of driverless vehicles outweigh the disadvantages?

With the rapid advancement of technology, driverless vehicles are expected to become common in the future. Although some people are concerned about the risks associated with autonomous transportation, I believe that the advantages of driverless cars, buses, and trucks outweigh the disadvantages because they can improve road safety and make transportation more convenient.

One major advantage of driverless vehicles is that they can significantly reduce road accidents. Most traffic accidents today are caused by human errors such as careless driving, speeding, or driving under the influence of alcohol. Autonomous vehicles, however, rely on advanced sensors and artificial intelligence, which can react more quickly and accurately than humans. For example, self-driving cars can automatically stop when detecting obstacles or sudden movements on the road, reducing the chances of collisions. Therefore, the widespread use of driverless vehicles could save thousands of lives and make roads much safer.

Another benefit is the increased convenience and efficiency that driverless transportation can provide. People would be able to use travel time more productively by working, relaxing, or even sleeping instead of concentrating on driving. In addition, driverless trucks and buses could operate continuously without needing long breaks, which may improve delivery services and public transport systems. For instance, companies could transport goods more quickly and reduce delays caused by driver fatigue. As a result, autonomous vehicles could improve both personal comfort and overall economic productivity.

In conclusion, although driverless vehicles may create concerns such as technological failures or job losses for drivers, their benefits are far more significant. By improving road safety and increasing transportation efficiency, autonomous vehicles are likely to have a positive impact on society in the future.

{{N}}.5 Model Essay – Internet and Plagiarism Writing

Essay Prompt

The internet has revolutionized how students access information, but it has also contributed to a significant increase in academic dishonesty, such as plagiarism. Do the disadvantages of the internet in education outweigh the advantages?

Model Answer (Band 7.0)

In the digital age, the internet has fundamentally transformed the educational landscape, providing students with immediate access to a vast repository of global information. While this technological shift has undeniably made academic dishonesty and plagiarism much easier, I firmly believe that the immense educational benefits of the internet far outweigh its drawbacks.

On the one hand, the primary disadvantage of the internet in education is the temptation and ease of plagiarism. Before the digital era, completing a high school report on a complex topic required genuine effort. For instance, a student writing a paper on environmental history would have had to quietly and thoroughly read a physical copy of a foundational book like Rachel Carson’s Silent Spring to formulate their own conclusions. Today, with a few clicks, students can simply copy pre-written analyses from websites. This reliance on readily available content has led to a rise in public accusations of plagiarism and a concerning decline in original, critical thinking among some learners.

On the other hand, the advantages provided by the internet are revolutionary and democratize education. The web breaks down geographical and financial barriers, allowing students from remote or underprivileged backgrounds to access digital libraries, academic journals, and lectures from world-renowned universities. Furthermore, the internet facilitates dynamic learning through educational videos, interactive forums, and global collaboration, which traditional textbooks simply cannot offer. The issue of plagiarism, while serious, can be effectively managed by modern institutions through the use of advanced plagiarism-detection software and by designing exam questions that require personalized, critical application of knowledge rather than simple memorization.

In conclusion, while the internet has inadvertently made academic dishonesty more accessible, its role as a powerful, equalizing educational tool is unparalleled. As long as schools adapt their assessment methods and utilize detection technologies, the advantages of digital learning significantly outweigh the disadvantages.

{{N}}.6 Đô thị hóa: di cư nông thôn – thành thị — bài luận mẫu Writing🆕

Many young people are leaving rural areas to study or work in cities. Do the advantages of this trend outweigh the disadvantages?

Moving to cities gives young people access to better education, career opportunities and modern infrastructure, helping them achieve career progression and financial stability that may be harder to reach in rural areas. However, this migration also causes overcrowding, job competition and rising living costs in cities, while rural areas suffer labour shortages and economic stagnation as families are separated. On balance, the benefits in education and career growth outweigh the drawbacks, but governments should invest in rural development to keep this growth balanced.
