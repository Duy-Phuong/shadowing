{{N}}.9 Advertisements and Children Part 3

Why are children often influenced by advertisements?

Children are often influenced by advertisements because they are less critical and more impressionable than adults. Bright colors, catchy music, and cartoon characters can easily capture their attention. In addition, children may not fully understand the persuasive purpose behind advertising. Compared to adults, they are more likely to believe promotional messages. Therefore, advertising can strongly shape children's preferences and desires.
